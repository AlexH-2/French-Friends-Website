Our Story

Founded by students, for students

French Friends started when a group of high school French Immersion students wanted to help younger kids fall in love with the language the way they did — through play, not worksheets. Every session is a casual, drop-off 1.5-hour block of games, crafts, and conversation, led by mentors who sat in the same seats not long ago.
